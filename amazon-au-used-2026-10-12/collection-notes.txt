Prices are AUD, as shown on the search listing cards. The used_price_shown_aud column copies the first price in the secondary offer section, normally labelled “More Buying Choices” or “No featured offers available”. Amazon labels these offers “used & new”, so this is the requested listing-page comparison price, not an independently verified used-only offer. The original label is included in each row.

new_price_aud is the main current product price shown on the listing card, not its RRP, typical price, crossed-out price, unit price, coupon price or instalment amount. These main listing prices have not each been independently verified against product offer conditions.
